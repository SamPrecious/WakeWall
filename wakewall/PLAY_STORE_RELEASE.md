# Google Play release notes

## Store classification

- Category: **Personalisation**
- Tags: **Wallpaper**, **Personalisation**, and **Theme**
- Optional tag: **Lock screen**, but only if lock-screen support is verified as reliable
- Use **Theme** because WakeWall lets people build themed wallpaper albums for
  different moods, seasons, and styles.

## Draft store listing

### Short description

> New wallpaper. New mood. Every time you wake your phone.

### Full description

Your phone doesn't have to look the same every time you pick it up.

Pick the photos you love, arrange them your way, and let WakeWall do the rest.
Each time you wake your phone, a new wallpaper is already waiting, as if your
screen changed its outfit while you weren't looking.

WakeWall began with a simple question: why couldn't Android show me a new
wallpaper every time I woke my phone, like iOS does? I couldn't find an app
that did it well, so I built one that feels modern and minimal, while keeping
the complicated parts out of sight.

Make it yours:

- Shuffle your wallpapers or play them in order
- Build themed albums for different moods, seasons or styles, then rotate the
  collections you feel like
- Crop, zoom and rotate each wallpaper
- Choose Fill, Fit or Blur for the perfect layout
- Reorder your queue, skip ahead or pause on a favourite
- Enable wallpaper scrolling across your Home screens
- Back up and restore your collection, crops, albums and settings

WakeWall only uses images you choose from your device. Everything stays local:
there are no accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers.

Sometimes, a new background is all it takes to make your phone feel new again.
